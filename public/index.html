Sweets

Thekua

Our Thekua is deep-fried in small batches using pure ghee and jaggery, exactly the way it's made for Chhath Puja back home. No refined sugar, no shortcuts — just wheat, jaggery and patience, giving it that authentic crunch-on-the-outside, soft-on-the-inside bite you won't find in a factory-made version.

Sweets

Anarsa

Made from hand-ground soaked rice and coated in real sesame seeds, our Anarsa follows the traditional Magahi recipe passed down through generations. It's slow-fermented for the right texture, then shallow-fried fresh to order — never sitting in a warehouse for weeks like mass-produced sweets.

Sweets

Khaja

Layer upon layer of delicate, flaky pastry soaked in sugar syrup — our Khaja is rolled by hand to get that signature crisp, airy texture. Every layer is proofed and fried with care, so it stays crunchy for weeks without losing its melt-in-the-mouth finish.

Namkeen

Namkeen Mixture

Our signature Bihari-style mixture blends crunchy sev, roasted lentils, peanuts and a house spice blend, deep-fried fresh every week — never pre-packed months in advance. It's the perfect tea-time companion, with a bold, tangy kick that keeps customers coming back for a refill.

Mithila · Madhubani

Made the way Madhubani is painted

The same corner of Bihar that gave the world Madhubani painting gave us our recipes. Both are made slowly, entirely by hand, and handed down through the women of the family — no stencils, no shortcuts, no two exactly alike.
