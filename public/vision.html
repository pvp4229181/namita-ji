Our Vision

Food that carries the feeling of home.

We want the warmth of Bihar's family kitchens to reach every table—through recipes made with care, familiar flavours and the joy of sharing.

Our story

Rooted in Bihar, made for every table.

Namita Ji is inspired by the kitchens where food is made patiently and shared generously. From Thekua prepared for Chhath to a tin of namkeen beside evening chai, these recipes hold memories as much as flavour.

We celebrate traditional Bihari snacks so that people who grew up with them can reconnect with a familiar taste, and people discovering them for the first time can feel warmly welcomed.

What guides us

Tradition, care and connection.

Our vision is to make regional food traditions easy to discover and joyful to share, while respecting the stories and care that make them special.
