Our Story

A family kitchen in Bihar, now reaching homes across India

From a Family Kitchen to Your Doorstep

Namita Ji began as a small family kitchen in Bihar, rooted in the belief that a mother's care belongs in every bite. What started with Thekua for Chhath Puja has grown into a home for authentic, preservative-free Bihari snacks — made the way our grandmothers made them.

Every batch is hand-prepared, roasted and packed fresh before it reaches your doorstep, wherever in India that may be. No shortcuts, no artificial preservatives — just the same recipes passed down through generations.
